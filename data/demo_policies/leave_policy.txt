ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY

Employees receive twenty paid annual leave days during each calendar year.

LEAVE CARRY-FORWARD

Employees may carry forward a maximum of five unused annual leave days into the following calendar year. Carried leave must be used before 31 March or it expires.

PROBATION

New employees may request paid annual leave after completing a six-month probation period. Managers may approve unpaid leave during probation in exceptional circumstances.

MEDICAL LEAVE

Medical leave lasting more than two consecutive working days requires a medical certificate.
