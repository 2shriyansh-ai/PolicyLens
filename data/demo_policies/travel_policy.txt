BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY

All business travel must be approved by the employee's manager before booking.

REIMBURSEMENT LIMITS

Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to INR 5,000 per night. Meals are reimbursed up to INR 1,000 per day. Local transport is reimbursed against valid receipts.

CLAIM DEADLINE

Expense claims must be submitted within thirty days after the employee returns from travel.
